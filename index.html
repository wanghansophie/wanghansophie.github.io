About

I am a Senior AI Scientist at LinkedIn, working on LLM post-training and agentic reinforcement learning. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Columbia University in 2024, where I was advised by Prof. James Anderson.

My research focuses on LLM post-training, federated learning, reinforcement learning, control theory, and large-scale optimization. The primary goal of my research is to enable reliable and efficient learning and decision-making in large-scale distributed systems.
